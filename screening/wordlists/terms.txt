# holds a photo). One term or phrase a line, matched after normalisation: NFKC, case folded,
# accents, look-alike letters (Cyrillic and Greek), leetspeak (n1gg3r) and spaced letters (n i g)
# undone. A word matches whole (with a plural s, es or z); in a [hold] check, any of these terms of
# 5 letters or more also matches inside a longer word. Numbers match only as whole numbers.
#
# [hide]       unambiguous slurs and extremist slogans: read clearly in the photo, or in its
#              title, the photo is hidden
# [hate]       hate words with other uses (history, film titles): they hold the photo; with a
#              confident hate symbol (screening.toml zeroshot.hide_hate_with_text) it is hidden
# [ambiguous]  codes with innocent uses (race numbers, model badges, the swastika characters,
#              read as "manjisign"): alone they do nothing (they are logged); with a
#              hate-symbol score of zeroshot.ambiguous_with_hate they hold
# [allow]      phrases that are no hit although they contain a listed term (removed from the
#              text before matching)
#
# Sources: ADL's hate symbols database (numeric codes, slogans) and the LDNOOBW English list.
